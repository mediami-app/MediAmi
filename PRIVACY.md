# MediAmi privacy policy

MediAmi does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. We have no
servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

The app connects only to the services you configure (for example Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr,
SABnzbd, Overseerr or Jellyseerr) using the address and API key you enter. Those details are kept
on your device, the keys in the system Keychain, and are sent only to the server they belong to.
What those servers store is governed by you and by them, not by us.

To show artwork the app loads images from the addresses your servers provide; when a server has
no copy of a cast photo, the image may be requested from its public source (TMDB's image host).
If you tap Play Trailer, the trailer plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, which is
subject to YouTube's own policy; nothing is requested from YouTube before you tap.

If you turn on download notifications, they are generated on your device from what your server
reports; no notification service of ours is involved.
